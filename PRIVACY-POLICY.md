# Privacy Policy — AI Diet & Nutrition Architect

**Effective date: June 15, 2025**

---

## Who we are

AI Diet & Nutrition Architect ("the App", "we", "our") is a mobile application that generates personalised daily diet plans using artificial intelligence, and helps users track their food intake and physical activity. The App is operated by Lakshmikanth, reachable at balamlakshmikanth9033@gmail.com.

---

## What information we collect and why

We collect only what is necessary to generate your personalised nutrition plan and improve your experience. We do not collect information for advertising or sell your data to any third party.

### Information you provide directly

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Account details** — name, mobile number (OTP login), or Google account (OAuth) | Create and identify your account |
| **Biometric data** — age, height, weight, gender | Calculate daily calorie and nutrition targets (BMR, TDEE) |
| **Health goals** — target weight, goal type, target duration | Adjust daily calorie budget toward your goal |
| **Food & dietary preferences** — cuisines, dietary type, medical conditions, allergies | Constrain AI meal plans so they are safe and appropriate for you |
| **Food logs** — food items logged manually or via photo | Track daily nutrition intake and build meal history |
| **Food photos** — photos taken or uploaded for AI food detection | Identify food and estimate nutrition via AI vision; stored linked to your food log entry |

### Information collected from your device

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Step count** — read from the built-in step counter sensor (Android Pedometer API) | Estimate daily calorie burn; only an aggregated daily estimate is sent to our backend — raw sensor data does not leave the device |
| **Sleep & activity data** (optional) — from a wearable via Android Health Connect | Enrich your activity dashboard; fully optional — the App works without it |

---

## How we use your information

- To generate your daily personalised AI diet plan based on your profile, goals, and preferences.
- To calculate and display your daily calorie and macro budget.
- To analyse food photos you submit and return nutritional estimates.
- To show you your meal history and logged food across past days.
- To authenticate your account securely.
- To improve the accuracy and quality of diet plans over time (using aggregated, anonymised data only).

We do **not** use your personal or health data for advertising, user profiling for third parties, or any purpose not described above.

---

## AI-generated content and medical disclaimer

The diet plans generated by this App are produced by an artificial intelligence model and are intended for **general wellness and nutrition guidance only**. They are **not medical advice** and should not replace consultation with a qualified doctor, dietitian, or healthcare professional — especially if you have a diagnosed medical condition such as diabetes, hypertension, PCOD, thyroid disorders, or any condition that requires clinical dietary management.

**Always consult your doctor before making significant changes to your diet**, particularly if you are on medication or managing a chronic health condition.

## Data sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the App:

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI / Anthropic** (AI model provider) | Generate diet plans and analyse food images | Profile data, goals, preferences, food photos |
| **Supabase** (database) | Store account data, diet plans, food logs, goals | All structured user data |
| **Cloudinary** (media storage) | Store and serve food photos | Food photos |
| **Firebase** (authentication) | Google OAuth login and mobile OTP | Phone number / Google account |
| **Sentry** (error monitoring) | Anonymised crash reports and error logs | No health data |

All providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the purpose of providing their service to us.

## Data storage and security

- Your data is stored on servers located outside India (Supabase, hosted on AWS).
- All data in transit is protected with HTTPS encryption.
- Authentication tokens are encrypted at rest.
- We implement industry-standard security practices appropriate for a consumer health application.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data, diet plans, food logs, and photos are **permanently deleted within 30 days**.
